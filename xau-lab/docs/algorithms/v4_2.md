# V4.2 — V4.1 primary + secondary breakout

Status: **experimental fractional version**

V4.2 is a real versioned experiment in the V4 family. It does **not** represent a new major-generation graduation. Major-number changes such as V5 remain subject to the stricter promotion bar; fractional versions are the lab's room for meaningful experimental iterations.

## Core idea

V4.2 keeps V4.1 intact as the slow primary engine, then adds a faster breakout engine only while neither V4.1 slow directional gate is active.

```text
V4.1 slow regime active?
    yes -> use V4.1 primary only
    no  -> secondary 120 s breakout may hunt
                ↓
        one shared position slot
                ↓
        same 3% planned risk / $4 stop
```

The ₹50/hour goal is diagnostic only. Missing the hourly target never forces a trade, loosens a filter, or increases risk.

## Primary engine

The primary path is the V4.1 trend/pullback/resumption logic:

- 1800-second momentum threshold ±$8;
- 300-second confirmation threshold ±$3;
- a real opposite 60-second pullback within the prior 300 seconds of at least $0.80;
- current 60-second resumption of at least $0.50;
- spread <= $0.30;
- 900-second primary cooldown;
- $4 stop;
- $21 take profit;
- 300-second zero-cross reversal exit;
- 1800-second maximum hold.

A sampled quote gap greater than 5 seconds resets feature continuity so momentum is not bridged across stale/session gaps.

## Secondary engine

Config hash: `f75134b98dc1`.

The secondary engine is permitted only when neither V4.1 slow directional gate is active.

It requires:

- prior 120-second mid-price channel, excluding the current observation;
- prior channel range >= $3.00;
- breakout >= $0.50 beyond that channel;
- aligned 30-second momentum >= $2.00 in magnitude;
- spread <= $0.30;
- 450-second secondary cooldown.

Exit:

- same $4 adverse stop;
- $12 take profit;
- 300-second momentum zero-cross against the position;
- 900-second maximum hold.

Only one position may be open across both engines.

## Hourly objective result

The experimental version improved activity without simply becoming pickier, but it remains far below the intended earning-velocity target:

- development average: ₹3.31 per observed hour;
- validation average: ₹5.88 per observed hour;
- constrained ex-post capture: 4.04% development / 6.74% validation;
- ₹50 reached in only 2/68 development hours and 2/22 validation hours.

The constrained opportunity ceiling uses hindsight and is a diagnostic ceiling, not an attainable trading forecast.

## Stress limitation

V4.2 remained positive under +10% spread, 1-second sampling, and adverse slippage through $0.10 per side in the research partitions.

At +25% spread:

- development: +₹25.39;
- validation: -₹25.93.

Cost sensitivity therefore remains a known weakness.

## Version policy

V4.2 is accepted as a **real fractional experimental version** because it is a distinct, measurable improvement over V4.1 in aggregate activity and replay P&L without increasing planned risk.

That acceptance does **not** consume the stricter graduation rule reserved for major generations such as V5.

The final 20% holdout remains unopened.

# V4.1 — medium-horizon trend / pullback / resumption

Status: **locked research candidate**

V4.1 is the first successor in this lab that produced positive development **and** validation results on the seven-day broker-tick study without touching the final holdout.

It is a substantial change from V4's short 2/5/15-second state machine.

## Core idea

Use a much slower directional context so the expected move is large relative to spread:

```text
30-minute trend
    ↓
5-minute trend agrees
    ↓
wait until a real 60-second pullback occurred
    ↓
require 60-second momentum to resume with trend
    ↓
enter
    ↓
3% risk-sized exposure
    ↓
deterministic stop / target / trend-reversal exit
```

## Entry

BUY requires:

- 1800-second momentum >= +$8;
- 300-second momentum >= +$3;
- during the previous 300 seconds, 60-second momentum reached <= -$0.80;
- current 60-second momentum recovered to >= +$0.50;
- spread <= $0.30;
- 900-second cooldown has elapsed.

SELL mirrors those conditions.

The pullback requirement prevents buying a mature uninterrupted burst. The longer horizons make a $0.20–$0.30 spread a much smaller fraction of the opportunity than in V3/V4.

## Position sizing

V4.1 stops sizing primarily from synthetic leverage.

Planned risk:

```text
risk_budget_inr = current_balance * 0.03
ounces_by_risk = risk_budget_inr / (4.0 * INR_PER_USD)
```

Exposure is still capped by the original 100x synthetic notional ceiling.

This position remains smaller than the observed broker minimum on a ₹500 account and is therefore still a **paper research size**, not directly executable live.

## Exit

A position closes on the first of:

- underlying XAU move <= -$4 from entry;
- underlying XAU move >= +$21 from entry;
- 300-second momentum crosses through zero against the trade;
- 1800-second maximum hold;
- challenge target / manual stop.

Risk is deterministic and local. No model/API inference is involved in exits.

## Why this was promoted

On the chronological development partition:

- ₹500 → **₹627.52**;
- +₹127.52 / +25.50%;
- 69 trades;
- profit factor **1.323**;
- max drawdown ₹87.29.

On the untouched-at-that-point validation partition:

- ₹500 → **₹538.79**;
- +₹38.79 / +7.76%;
- 8 trades;
- profit factor **1.811**;
- max drawdown ₹40.18.

The development dates were also positive when independently reset to ₹500.

Stress tests remained positive under:

- observed spread +10%;
- adverse execution slippage up to $0.10 per side;
- 1-second sampling instead of 500 ms.

A +25% spread stress remained approximately flat on development but failed validation, so transaction-cost sensitivity is still a known weakness.

## Important limitation

This is **not proof of profitability**.

Validation has only eight trades, and the locked holdout has not been evaluated. V4.1 is promoted because it crossed the lab's current "worth further testing" threshold, not because it is production-ready.

# V4.3 — locked regime-adaptive composite

Status: **locked fractional research version**

V4.3 locks the strongest fixed regime-adaptive candidate from the 2026-09-24 capture-target study.

It did **not** reach the aspirational 20% capture target. It is locked because it is a distinct fractional improvement over V4.2 and the user explicitly chose to preserve this candidate as V4.3 rather than discard it.

Any parameter or structural changes after this lock become **V4.4**.

## Core architecture

V4.3 keeps the V4.1/V4.2 two-engine structure and adds a high-activity regime router.

```text
tick stream
    ↓
range300 + ER60
    ↓
NORMAL regime ──→ V4.2 behavior
HIGH regime   ──→ shorter cooldowns + more permissive secondary breakout
                  + alternate position-management rules
```

High activity is defined as:

- 300-second mid-price range >= $5;
- 60-second efficiency ratio >= 0.03.

The efficiency ratio is the absolute 60-second net move divided by the sum of absolute sampled mid-price changes over the same window.

## Primary engine

The primary entry remains V4.1:

- 1800-second momentum >= +$8 / <= -$8;
- 300-second momentum agrees by at least $3;
- a real opposite 60-second pullback of at least $0.80 occurred in the previous 300 seconds;
- current 60-second momentum resumed by at least $0.50;
- spread <= $0.30.

Normal-regime behavior remains:

- 900-second cooldown;
- $21 take profit;
- $4 stop;
- 300-second momentum zero-cross reversal exit;
- 1800-second max hold.

High-activity primary behavior:

- cooldown reduced to 450 seconds;
- $25 take-profit cap;
- $4 stop unchanged;
- max hold reduced to 900 seconds;
- momentum-reversal exit disabled.

## Secondary engine

V4.3 parity correction: the locked research row applies the V4.3 breakout geometry **globally**, not only in the high-activity state:

- 120-second prior channel;
- channel range >= $4;
- breakout buffer = max($0.50, 0.02 × channel range);
- |30-second momentum| >= $1.50.

Routing still changes by regime:

- outside high activity, secondary entry remains blocked while the V4.1 slow directional gate is active and uses a 450-second cooldown;
- in high activity, secondary entry may operate inside the slow gate and cooldown is reduced to 90 seconds;
- $8 take-profit cap;
- $4 stop unchanged;
- max hold 1200 seconds;
- momentum-reversal exit disabled;
- trail parameters retained at +$12 trigger / $3 give-back. Under the locked $8 take-profit cap, that trail is normally shadowed by the earlier take-profit exit; the values are retained because they are part of the recorded search configuration.

One position may be open across both engines.

## Locked research evidence

The canonical V4.3 aggregate was **not** a literal one-pass continuous replay. It was calculated by resetting each of the five chronological research segments to ₹500 and then compounding the five segment returns in order. The recorded segment P&Ls were:

- +₹530.62;
- -₹3.86;
- -₹7.15;
- +₹5.07;
- +₹41.90.

Compounding those reset-segment returns produces:

- net P&L: **+₹603.60**;
- final synthetic balance: **₹1,103.60**;
- profit factor: **1.456**;
- trades: **114**;
- max drawdown: **₹257.52**;
- best trade: **+₹173.16**;
- worst trade: **-₹34.57**;
- constrained ex-post opportunity capture: **8.07%**.

The same first-80% research pool had a constrained ex-post opportunity ceiling of ₹7,482.60.

For comparison, V4.2 produced about +₹412.58 and ~5.51% capture when run continuously over the same research pool.

## Follow-up rejection

A later exploratory exit-harvest tweak that widened the high-regime take-profit levels looked stronger in aggregate but underperformed V4.3 on the internal 60–80% validation band and both subfolds. That tweak was rejected.

Therefore the 8.07% adaptive candidate, not the later $30/$15 exit-harvest variant, is the configuration locked as V4.3.

## Limitations

- The aspirational >=20% capture target was not reached.
- Max drawdown increased materially versus V4.2.
- The first 80% pool was heavily used for research and selection.
- The final 20% holdout remains unopened.
- The 8.07% result is a research replay result, not proof of future profitability.

## V4.5-C Phase C1 — completed negative result

Phase C1 kept Candidate B admission unchanged and searched BURST lifecycle behavior for better profit velocity and lower sampling fragility.

It tested 48 combinations of:

- max hold: 120 / 180 s;
- trail trigger: $5.50 / $6.50 / $7.50;
- trail give-back: $2.00 / $2.50;
- optional raw-flow continuation extension;
- optional flow-decay early exit.

All 48 seed variants reproduced Candidate B exactly at both 500 ms and 1 s.

The top eight chronological evaluation candidates and top three full first-80% summaries also reproduced Candidate B exactly.

Therefore Phase C1 produced **no distinct Candidate C** and no promotion.

The result indicates that these lifecycle controls are shadowed/inactive on the observed BURST trade paths. Earlier BURST exit conditions and/or entry timing dominate before the tested knobs can change realized outcomes.

## Next V4.5 research direction

Do not run another blind grid over the same max-hold/trail/flow-decay controls.

First instrument the actual BURST path at trade level:

- exact exit reason;
- entry/exit timing;
- MFE/MAE and time-to-MFE;
- state at exit;
- short-window post-exit favorable excursion;
- 500 ms versus 1 s opportunity matching/missing.

The next candidate should be driven by the dominant observed exit/entry failure mode.

The 1 s grid remains a robustness diagnostic rather than the intended trading cadence, but a materially stronger 1 s result is desirable because it indicates less dependence on sub-second sampling.

## Search discipline

- first 80% research pool only;
- 0-40% used for search/selection;
- later chronological segments used for evaluation;
- final 20% holdout remains sealed;
- 3% planned risk remains fixed;
- $4 emergency stop remains fixed unless a future documented research plan explicitly changes it;
- one shared position slot remains in force;
- no candidate is promoted from search score alone;
- execution-cost stress is required before any V4.5 lock.

## Lock direction

A strong V4.5 lock candidate should move toward:

- roughly 20% or better 500 ms capture if achieved without fragility;
- materially reduced 500 ms -> 1 s degradation, with ~10% 1 s capture treated as an aspirational robustness level rather than a forced threshold;
- higher profit velocity than Candidate B;
- no material PF or drawdown deterioration;
- improvement or containment of the weak 50-60% regime;
- positive cost-stress behavior.

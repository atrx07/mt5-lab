## Why the re-baseline was necessary

Earlier experiments used multiple temporary replay implementations. They differed subtly in:

- quote bucketing;
- raw-flow feature construction;
- sampled continuity handling;
- exact rolling-window behavior;
- reset/compound versus one-pass accounting.

That allowed the same locked strategy to acquire different reported baselines.

The canonical replay harness freezes all of those choices and rejects a candidate run immediately if the V4.4 baseline drifts.
